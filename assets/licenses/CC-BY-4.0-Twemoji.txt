Twemoji graphics

The emoji pictures on EasyPlay's Chat page are Twemoji graphics, shipped inside the
application (they are never loaded from a server).

Copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors.
Graphics licensed under CC-BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji

They reach EasyPlay through the twemoji-assets crate (MIT), unchanged except that pictures
may be shown smaller or in grey (the emoji picker's group tabs).
